## MRT

The MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) is one of the main ways to travel around Singapore. It is fast, reliable and covers many important areas and tourist attractions.

The MRT and LRT systems form the backbone of Singapore's public transport network.

## Public Buses

Singapore has an extensive bus network connecting many parts of the island. Buses can be useful when an attraction is not immediately next to an MRT station.

Buses also provide an opportunity to see the city while travelling.

## Paying for Public Transport

Visit Singapore states that travellers can use contactless bank cards, mobile wallets, EZ-Link cards, cash in applicable situations, or a Singapore Tourist Pass.

The Singapore Tourist Pass provides unlimited travel on eligible basic public transport services for its validity period.

## Taxis and Private Hire

Taxis are metered and can be booked through mobile applications or found at designated taxi stands.

Ride-hailing/private-hire options are also available through mobile applications.

## Walking

Singapore is pedestrian-friendly in many central areas. Walking can be useful for exploring neighbourhoods such as Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Glam.

Because Singapore has a tropical climate, visitors should consider heat, humidity, water and occasional rain when planning long walking routes.

## Transport Planning

For route planning, travellers can use Singapore's public transport applications and mapping applications to identify MRT and bus routes.

For a typical sightseeing trip, MRT combined with walking is a practical option, while buses and taxis/private-hire vehicles can be useful when they provide more convenient access to specific destinations.
