## General Itinerary Planning

Singapore is compact enough that travellers can combine several attractions and neighbourhoods in a single day. A good itinerary can combine attractions based on geographical proximity and traveller interests.

## Sample Day 1 — Marina Bay and Urban Nature

### Morning
Explore the Marina Bay area.

### Afternoon
Visit Gardens by the Bay, including the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest.

### Evening
Explore Marina Bay Sands and the surrounding Marina Bay area.

## Sample Day 2 — Sentosa and Family Activities

### Morning
Travel to Sentosa.

### Afternoon
Explore family-oriented attractions and entertainment on Sentosa.

### Evening
Enjoy the beaches or other evening activities on the island.

## Sample Day 3 — Culture and Local Food

### Morning
Explore Chinatown and its cultural attractions.

### Afternoon
Visit Little India or Kampong Glam.

### Evening
Experience local food at a hawker centre.

## Alternative Cultural Day

A cultural itinerary can combine:
- Chinatown
- Little India
- Kampong Glam
- Heritage sites
- Local food experiences

## Family Itinerary

A family-oriented itinerary can combine:
- Gardens by the Bay
- Sentosa
- Singapore Zoo / Mandai attractions
- Indoor attractions
- Family-friendly food experiences

## Indoor/Outdoor Planning

Singapore's tropical weather means outdoor activities should be planned with flexibility.

If heavy rain is forecast:
- Replace long outdoor walks with indoor attractions.
- Consider Cloud Forest and Flower Dome.
- Consider museums and indoor cultural attractions.
- Consider indoor entertainment attractions.

If weather is suitable:
- Prioritise Gardens by the Bay outdoor areas.
- Explore neighbourhoods on foot.
- Visit Sentosa beaches.
- Consider outdoor parks and nature attractions.

## Seven-Day Itinerary Reference

Visit Singapore's seven-day itinerary includes:
- Day 1: Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay Sands
- Day 2: Sentosa
- Additional days covering different parts of Singapore and different experiences.

The seven-day itinerary can be used as a source of ideas rather than as a fixed itinerary for every traveller.
