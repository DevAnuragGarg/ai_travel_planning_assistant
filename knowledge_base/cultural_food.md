## Languages

Singapore has four official languages:
- English
- Malay
- Mandarin
- Tamil

English is widely used and is a common language for visitors.

## Climate

Singapore has a tropical climate throughout the year. It is generally hot and humid, and rain can occur even during otherwise suitable travel days.

Visitors should consider lightweight clothing, water and an umbrella when planning outdoor activities.

## Drinking Water

Visit Singapore states that tap water in Singapore is safe to drink.

## Payments

Credit cards and contactless payment methods are widely accepted. Mobile wallets and other electronic payment methods are also available.

## Tipping

Tipping is not traditionally customary in Singapore, although visitors may choose to tip for good service.

## Food Culture

Singapore has a multicultural food culture influenced by Chinese, Malay, Indian and other culinary traditions.

Hawker centres are an important part of the local food experience and provide a wide variety of relatively affordable food.

## Local Food Experiences

Visitors can explore different food traditions in neighbourhoods such as:
- Chinatown — Chinese food and heritage
- Little India — Indian cuisine
- Kampong Glam / Geylang Serai — Malay and Muslim food culture
- East Coast — seafood and Peranakan influences

Common Singapore food experiences include hawker food and local dishes such as:
- Chicken rice
- Laksa
- Chilli crab
- Nasi briyani
- Satay

## Hawker Centre Etiquette

Visitors should return trays and crockery to designated return stations after eating at hawker centres, food courts and coffeeshops.

## Cultural Exploration

For cultural experiences, visitors can explore:
- Chinatown
- Little India
- Kampong Glam
- Civic District
- Tiong Bahru
- Geylang Serai

These areas provide different perspectives on Singapore's multicultural history and communities.

## Practical Advice

Singapore's tropical climate means outdoor itineraries should allow flexibility for heat and rain.

Indoor alternatives are useful when heavy rain occurs.
