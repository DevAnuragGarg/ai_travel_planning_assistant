## Major Attractions

### Gardens by the Bay
Gardens by the Bay is a major attraction in Singapore's Marina Bay area. It includes the Flower Dome, Cloud Forest and Supertree Grove. It combines gardens, conservatories and futuristic architecture.

### Marina Bay
Marina Bay is an iconic area containing attractions such as Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, the Singapore Flyer and the Esplanade Theatres.

### Sentosa
Sentosa is a resort island south of Singapore known for beaches, family-friendly activities and entertainment attractions including Universal Studios Singapore.

### Singapore Botanic Gardens
The Singapore Botanic Gardens is a major nature attraction and is Singapore's UNESCO World Heritage Site.

### Mandai Wildlife Reserve
The Mandai area contains major wildlife attractions including Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, Bird Paradise and River Wonders.

### Chinatown
Chinatown is a Chinese heritage area with restored shophouses, cultural attractions and food experiences.

### Little India
Little India is a culturally distinctive neighbourhood centred around Serangoon Road. It is known for Indian food, shops and cultural attractions.

### Kampong Glam
Kampong Glam is associated with Singapore's Malay heritage and is located around the Bugis area.

### Orchard Road
Orchard Road is Singapore's major shopping district, with many shopping malls and retail experiences.
